Common Platform Deductions Decoded

Where your payout gets quietly trimmed. Understanding deduction architecture helps you protect against unfair rating clawbacks and dead fuel burn.

cancel

Customer Cancellation Penalties

Platforms frequently push customer disputes, unexpected restaurant delays, or false drop claims onto delivery partners as penalties per incident.

Impact: ₹300–₹800/wk
Illustrative example, not platform data.
pie_chart

Dynamic Platform Commission Slices

Commission structures vary dynamically based on peak-hour zones, marketplace liquidity algorithms, and tier requirements.

Impact: 8%–25% gross
Illustrative example, not platform data.
local_gas_station

Empty kilometres driven back to base

(Uncompensated deadheading)

Empty kilometers driven back to commercial restaurant clusters or dark stores after drops are 100% driver-funded fuel with zero tariff return.

Impact: 20%–35% fuel loss
Illustrative example, not platform data.
checkroom

Uniform & Asset Amortization

Initial kit onboarding costs, bag replacement deductions, and maintenance fees amortized quietly across weekly settlement cycles.

Impact: ₹150–₹350/mo
